How to read this table
  • Total / Σ Amount — receipts of that payment type in the MIS, and their rupee value.
  • Matched — found in the bank statement / MPR by the matching rules. Gateway — matched through the EaseBuzz gateway ID. Contra — cheques accounted for by the refund document.
  • Partial — part of a grouped match; Mismatch — reference found but the amount differs; Unmatched — no counterpart found in a statement that does cover that date.
  • Awaiting — no bank / MPR statement covers that date yet (or it is within the settlement days of the statement's last date). Not a mismatch: these reconcile on their own once the statement is in.
  • Balance Amount — shortfall where a grouped match came up short of its bank credit. 0.00 means nothing pending; means money still to be accounted for.
  • Reconciled — (Matched + Gateway + Contra) ÷ (Total − Awaiting). Green 95%+, amber 80–95%, red below 80%.
